# Cocktail story notes

The 107 bilingual notes in `src/cocktail-stories.json` are original editorial summaries, separate from recipe measures. They cover all 102 current recipes and the five legacy favorites.

- `history`: a short historical account, with attribution qualified where appropriate.
- `disputed`: competing origin claims or popular anecdotes; no single inventor is presented as settled fact.
- `context`: the name, regional tradition, recipe family or style, rather than an invented creation story.

A public secondary compilation was read for the matching drinks: [Drinko history](https://github.com/cilippofilia/drinko-learn/blob/9575611922024631e79007e3ba7b8ece7f1cf3f7/cocktails/history/en/history.json), pinned to commit `9575611922024631e79007e3ba7b8ece7f1cf3f7`. It is not treated as primary authority: precise dates and colorful anecdotes are not imported wholesale, and competing claims remain qualified. The other notes use concise editorial background and named traditions. No third-party prose or images are reproduced.

Drinko cross-check coverage (28 entries): `negroni`, `margarita`, `mojito`, `whiskey-sour`, `daiquiri`, `espresso-martini`, `old-fashioned`, `manhattan`, `tommy-s-margarita`, `caipirinha`, `clover-club`, `mimosa`, `french-75`, `pina-colada`, `bellini`, `spritz-veneziano`, `hemingway-special`, `americano`, `bloody-mary`, `boulevardier`, `champagne-cocktail`, `cosmopolitan`, `mai-tai`, `mint-julep`, `paloma`, `pisco-sour`, `porn-star-martini`, `zombie`.

Direct access to Wikipedia, Difford’s Guide and the IBA website was unavailable in this environment. Wikipedia links are explicitly labeled **search / further reading**, not citations to pages verified live. Context notes also link to the recipe snapshot used by the app. These links do not imply that IBA verified the editorial stories. Further fact checking should use original publications and specialist historical research before treating a contested date or author as definitive.
